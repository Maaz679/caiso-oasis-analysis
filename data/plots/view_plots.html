Compares prices across three major CAISO trading hubs: NP15 (Northern CA), SP15 (Southern CA), and ZP26 (San Diego). Price differences indicate transmission congestion.

3. Fuel Mix Generation Stack

Stacked area chart showing generation by fuel type (top) and battery storage activity (bottom). Notice the "duck curve" - batteries charge during high solar and discharge during evening peak.

4. Average Generation by Fuel Type

Average generation mix showing California's high renewable penetration. Solar leads at ~30%, followed by imports and hydro.
